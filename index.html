Welcome to JMHAA

The official alumni association of John Marshall Harlan High School

The John Marshall Harlan High School Alumni Association, NFP, is dedicated to connecting alumni, supporting current students through scholarships, and preserving the rich history of our beloved school in the Roseland community.

Whether you graduated last year or fifty years ago, your Falcon pride lives on. Join us in celebrating our past, supporting our present, and investing in the future of Harlan.
