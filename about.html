About the Association

Preserving our legacy, supporting our students, and uniting the Falcon family since 2001.

Our Mission & Vision

The John Marshall Harlan High School Alumni Association, NFP, is a non-profit organization dedicated to connecting alumni, supporting current students through scholarships, and preserving the rich history of our beloved school.

Founded in December 2001 and incorporated under the laws of the State of Illinois in May 2001, we maintain a single, unified identity. We are not affiliated with any for-profit organizations or individual classes using the John Marshall Harlan High School name.

Our vision is to ensure that every Falcon, regardless of their graduating class, feels connected to their roots and empowered to give back to the Roseland community.

Our Roots in Roseland

John Marshall Harlan Community Academy High School is a public 4-year high school and middle school located in the Roseland community area on the South Side of Chicago. For decades, Harlan has been a beacon of education, resilience, and community pride.

Building the Future

Through our initiatives, we aim to bridge the gap between our proud past and our promising future. By funding scholarships, hosting career days, and maintaining a strong alumni network, we ensure that the Falcon spirit continues to soar for generations to come.
