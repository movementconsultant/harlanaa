Our Commitment to Transparency

The John Marshall Harlan High School Alumni Association, NFP, is a registered non-profit organization incorporated under the laws of the State of Illinois. We maintain a single, unified identity and are not affiliated with any for-profit organizations or individual classes using the John Marshall Harlan High School name.

All donations are utilized strictly to further our mission of supporting Harlan students, alumni, and community programs.
